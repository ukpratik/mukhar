- Words has three parts: Learn new words, Revise the ones you know, and My words
- Learn: one card at a time with the meaning in English and Hindi, an example and the pronunciation; Got it, Star, Later or Not for me
- Revise: see the word, recall the meaning, then Knew it or Again; words come back when it's time
- Starred and Done lists; discarded words can be brought back
- 5 word cards a day count for your streak
